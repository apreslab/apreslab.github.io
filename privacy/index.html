APRÈS LAB / YOUR DATA

Privacy
Policy.

This policy covers our website and its contact form.

Last updated: October 26, 2026

We believe clarity matters. Here is how we handle information when you visit our website or contact our studio.

  1. 01

    Who is responsible?

    This website is operated by APRÈS LAB, an independent creative studio based in Buenos Aires, Argentina.

    For questions about personal data, or to request the full legal identity and postal contact details of the responsible party, write to apreslabcontact@gmail.com.

  2. 02

    Information we collect

    When you use the contact form, we ask for your name, email address, phone number and message. All four fields are required to submit the form. You may choose to contact us directly by email instead.

    Please do not include sensitive information or confidential material that we have not specifically requested.

    When you visit our site, technical data such as your IP address may be processed by hosting and resource providers. GitHub Pages states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security.

  3. 03

    Why we use it

    We use inquiry details to reply to your message, understand a possible project or collaboration, and manage related professional correspondence. The form also uses technical anti-spam measures.

    Sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing newsletter. We do not use this form as an advertising or profiling tool.

  4. 04

    Service providers

    FormSubmit processes and forwards your inquiry to our email inbox. We receive correspondence through Google’s Gmail service. GitHub Pages hosts the site, and Google serves the Archivo font through Google Fonts.

    FormSubmit may use anti-spam systems, including reCAPTCHA. These providers may process technical or submission data outside Argentina under their respective practices and policies.

  5. 05

    How long we keep it

    FormSubmit’s documentation states that it retains form submissions for 30 days. This is separate from copies delivered to our Gmail inbox.

    We keep inquiry correspondence only while it is needed to answer your request, continue related discussions or meet an applicable legal obligation. There is no published fixed retention period for mailbox copies; you can request deletion by emailing us, subject to applicable exceptions.

  6. 06

    Your choices and rights

    You can request access to, correction, updating or deletion of personal data relating to you by writing to apreslabcontact@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

    The contact form fields are required for that channel; if you prefer not to provide a phone number, you can email us directly. If information is missing or inaccurate, we may be unable to respond appropriately.

    For information about the rights recognized in Argentina, visit the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública.

  7. 07

    Cookies and updates

    As currently built, this website does not include our own analytics tags, advertising pixels or optional marketing cookies. External providers may use technical identifiers or cookies according to their policies; FormSubmit’s anti-spam mechanisms may involve their own services.

    If the site or our practices change, we may update this notice and revise the date shown above.
